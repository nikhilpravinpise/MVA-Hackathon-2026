## Key findings (all regenerated, nothing hand-typed)

**PRISM x aneuploidy** (1,498 compounds, 588 launched broad_ids, ~457
lines/regression):

- **Validation**: my per-drug t-statistics reproduce Cohen-Sharir's own
  PRISM aneuploidy analysis (Supp Table 5) with **Spearman rho = +0.719,
  p = 2e-229, sign concordance 80%** across 1,442 matched drugs. Sign
  convention verified directly on nutlin-3/danusertib (their
  effect_size > 0 = aneuploid-RESISTANT).
- **No launched drug is FDR-significant for aneuploidy-selectivity**
  (min q = 0.069). Top directional hits (hypothesis-generating only):
  ingenol-mebutate, lomefloxacin, lenalidomide, imatinib, dasatinib.
- **SAC/mitotic drugs are mostly aneuploid-RESISTANT in this screen**
  (42/50 beta > 0), matching Cohen-Sharir's short-term-exposure result.
  Meanwhile BUB1B is itself a top aneuploidy-associated *genetic*
  dependency (Supp Table 2: DRIVE-RNAi effect -0.317 q = 1.1e-4;
  Achilles-RNAi -0.138 q = 5.7e-4) - the drug-vs-gene asymmetry their
  paper reported. Supports putting SAC inhibitors in the
  considered-and-rejected table.
- **Low-BUB1B sensitivity**: nothing significant among launched drugs
  (min q = 0.216); thesis-consistent direction for MDM2i (+0.037 to
  +0.045) and dasatinib (+0.037). Opposite direction: PLK1/Eg5
  inhibitors (ispinesib q = 0.04) kill *high*-BUB1B lines, consistent
  with BUB1B as a proliferation marker.
- **Coverage caveat**: metformin, chloroquine, hydroxychloroquine,
  reversine are NOT in the secondary screen; sirolimus/everolimus show
  no selective signal. L3 must not cite PRISM evidence for absent drugs.

**ESM-2 N1002K** (model `esm2_t30_150M_UR50D`, CPU, masked-marginal LLR,
1022-aa windows, n = 1,319 variants scored):

- **N1002K LLR = -0.369**: 52nd percentile of deleteriousness vs all
  1,273 gnomAD-observed BUB1B missense; 39th vs the ClinVar+gnomAD-common
  benign panel. Mildly deleterious, mid-distribution - consistent with a
  *hypomorph* (partial function retained), not a null. Interpretation
  recorded in esm.json.
- Calibration: ClinVar LP missense Q467H LLR = -0.826 (more damaging
  than N1002K); nonsense-proxy median -8.90 (constraint confirmed,
  AUC = 1.0 as sanity); gnomAD common vs ultra-rare separation weak
  (AUC 0.50 at 150M scale - the 650M Modal run may separate better).
- ClinVar holds exactly **one** classified pathogenic BUB1B missense
  (Q467H): a missense-only AUC is not computable - stated plainly in
  esm.json rather than padded with proxies.
- 16/1,273 gnomAD rows dropped: their hgvsp is not MANE-consistent
  (pos 87-94 cluster, non-MANE transcript annotation); all logged in
  `esm.json.dropped_variants`. MANE numbering verified: O60566 ==
  ENSP00000287598.7, pos 737 = L, pos 1002 = N. N1002K itself is in
  gnomAD r4 (exome AC = 1, AF = 6.8e-7) - flagged and excluded from the
  percentile background.
