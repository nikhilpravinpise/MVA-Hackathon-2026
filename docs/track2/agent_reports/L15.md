## r2 - coverage detector

- `%` moved outside the `\b` unit group (a word boundary can never
  follow a non-word char - the exact bug the reviewer demonstrated).
- `_MECH_VERBS` 14 lemmas -> ~60: suppresses, clears,
  stabilises/destabilises, degrades, elevates, lowers, prevents,
  worsens, rescues, restores, blocks, blunts, induces, promotes,
  enhances, disrupts, impairs, attenuates, potentiates, depletes,
  ameliorates, exacerbates, mimics, reverses, corrects, extends,
  shortens, delays, accelerates, kills, sensitises, drives, triggers,
  mediates, eliminates, removes, protects, damages, weakens,
  strengthens, arrests, modulates, down/upregulates, treats, decays -
  each with s/ed/ing conjugations.
- Unitless numerics: hyphenated `N-fold` ("a 3-fold drop"),
  `N of M` ("12 of 96"), bare sub-1 statistics (`0.72`, `0.05`,
  `q 0.45`; normal decimals `12.5`/`3.11` and integers stay unclaimed).
- Two honest carve-outs added alongside (documented in code):
  bare `0.x` decimals alone don't trigger in *table rows* (score/weight
  data cells - the reviewer's decimal proofs were prose; `29.9%` and
  `12 of 96` still fire in tables), and verb-only triggers don't fire
  on *column-header rows* (a header labels metrics; its data rows are
  the claims).
- `redteam2.md` added to `COVERAGE_SKIP_BASENAMES` (review notes like
  `redteam.md`, not report prose).
- `main()` now reconfigures stdout `errors="replace"` - the summary
  crashed on cp1252 consoles printing '∩' claims.
- Tests: every reviewer proof sentence asserted as a trigger, plus
  non-trigger negatives (versions, dates, PMIDs, plain counts).

## r4 - pediatric flags

Both offered behaviors implemented (they answer different questions):

- **`unknown` tier**: a label silent on pediatrics (or no label) is
  `unknown`, not `none` - `none` now requires a formal denial in
  pediatric-population text (denial-priority: positive > denial >
  descriptive > silent). Descriptive pediatric text without
  indication/denial is `pediatric_text_only`.
- **`_exists_:pediatric_use` deep scan**: whenever the picked label is
  not pediatric-positive, one extra `(generic OR substance) AND
  _exists_:pediatric_use limit=25` query over the fully-stripped name;
  the covering single-ingredient label with the highest
  pediatric-informativeness wins (positive > descriptive > negated >
  silent; unqualified generics before route-qualified). Disclosed in
  `approval.openfda_match`. The plain `limit=1` query URL is
  byte-identical - zero fixture-key churn; the deep query is additive.
- Recorded evidence: acetaminophen recovers
  `labeled_pediatric_indication` (injection label 0f67c1bd, ">=2
  years"), cyclosporine recovers `pediatric_text_only` (transplant
  label 5e5926a7, "as young as 6 months"), clotrimazole and
  methylprednisolone re-pick positive labels; negated-only results
  (nicotine "not recommended") stay `none`; silent OTC labels
  (glycerin, flutamide, 11 no-label candidates) move `none` -> `unknown`.

## n4 - token-exact matching

`openfda_pick_single`/`_actives_cover`: token-set equality after
dropping `_FORM_TOKENS` (salts/hydrates/esters incl. hippurate,
mepesuccinate). "SIROLIMUS" can no longer match "TEMSIROLIMUS",
"INSULIN" cannot match "INSULIN LISPRO"/"INSULIN GLARGINE"; "METFORMIN
HYDROCHLORIDE" still matches "metformin". Tests cover both directions.

## n3 - word-boundary clip

`_clip_words(text, 240)` normalizes whitespace, cuts at the last space
inside the limit, strips trailing ` ,;:-(`, appends `" ..."` - same
convention as the report generator's `clip()`. 12 committed evidence
texts get cleaner cuts.
