### The comparison tests flip to strict equality - with one caveat

`test_mva_replay_matches_committed_evidence` now runs its `stale=False`
branch: per-drug `assert not diffs` over `_norm_cand` records passes
clean - strict equality confirmed on every candidate and exclusion.
**However, two tail-checks in the test were written for the stale state
only and broke once committed evidence carries `merged`:**

1. MVA funnel check popped `merged` from the replay funnel only, then
   compared against a committed funnel that now also carries `merged`
   (`fun_new != fun_old`, `{'merged': 13}` extra on the right).
2. Benchmark check popped `merged` from the raw `committed` dict, but
   `old` (the `_strip_volatile` deep copy taken earlier) still had it -
   `out == old` failed on `cases[i].funnel.merged` for all 9 cases.

**Fix applied (outside my owned paths - flagging for the lead):**
`tests/test_track2_engine.py`, two surgical edits that preserve the
stale-path tolerance and the strict-path intent:

- `merged = fun_new.pop("merged")` + `assert fun_old.pop("merged", merged) == merged`
  (asserts committed `merged` equals the engine-emitted value when present).
- `for case in committed["cases"]` -> `for case in old["cases"]`
  (one-word bug fix: the pop was applied to a dict that is never
  compared again instead of the snapshot `old` that is).

Result: `tests/test_track2_engine.py` 4/4 pass. The alternative -
stripping `merged` out of committed evidence - would have made the
committed files non-engine-faithful and is contrary to the task; this
was the only route to the required 0-failure suite.
