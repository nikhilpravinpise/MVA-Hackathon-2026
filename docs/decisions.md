# Decision log

Decision-freeze ledger (plan.md section 2.1.1): any post-freeze change to
`config/pipeline.yaml` or to load-bearing pipeline strategy gets a row here
with date, the decision, its justification, and the alternatives considered.
Rows are append-only; corrections go in new rows, never edits.

| Date | Decision | Justification | Alternatives considered |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-26 | Added `scoring.epcr_decay: 0.55` to config/pipeline.yaml | Config stated epcr is "a monotone map of rank into (0,1]" (epcr_top 0.95, epcr_floor 0.05) without naming a functional form; a geometric decay `epcr_i = epcr_floor + (epcr_top - epcr_floor) * 0.55^(rank-1)` (rank 1 always = epcr_top, asymptote = epcr_floor) is the simplest monotone map reaching ~floor within the 10-row cap. Committed BEFORE any patient-data access, so it is pre-registration, not post-hoc tuning. | Linear decay (needs an arbitrary step size); rank-percentile map (couples epcr to row count, weaker top-row signal); leave unspecified (violates freeze - the writer would pick ad hoc at CSV time) |
