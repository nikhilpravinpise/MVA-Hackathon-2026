<h1 align="center">

  🧬 Rare Disease, Real Kid: The MVA Hackathon 2026

</h1>

The genome and clinical story here belong to a real child living with Mosaic Variegated
Aneuploidy (MVA), an ultra-rare genetic condition affecting fewer than 50 people worldwide.
There is currently no established treatment, and care today means managing symptoms.

The family has opened the case to the research community, hoping someone can find an answer.

<h3 align="center">Help us understand MVA better!</h3>
